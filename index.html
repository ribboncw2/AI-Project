How to use this

You do not need to write code. This page helps you assemble one professional report that a principal and a board reader can each use. Wording changes happen in Cursor chat; this site is for reading, files, preview, and print.

Three steps

  1. Keep this site open at http://localhost:8080 (start it with start.command in the AI-Project folder).
  2. On Uploads, add files you downloaded from Google Drive (SIP excerpts, rubrics, unit plans). Do not put student names in file titles if you can help it.
  3. In Cursor chat, say what to add, change, or rephrase. Then open Preview (Principal, Board, or Full) and use Print → Save as PDF.
Principal

School version

What you tried in class, evidence of learning (for / as / of), school improvement fit, and what support you need from the school.

Board

System version

Why it matters beyond one room: equity of access, well-being, curriculum implementation, resources, and a clear next action.

Privacy

Stays on this Mac

Uploads are not sent to GitHub. The public repo holds this dashboard only. No Google Drive login is connected.

Ontario language used here

Growing Success; overall and specific expectations; assessment for, as, and of learning; success criteria and descriptive feedback; Universal Design for Learning and differentiation; equity and inclusive education; OCT Standards of Practice. Not US “standards-based grading” wording.
